# Archived training corpora

- `champion_snapshots_pre_searchfix_gen140.csv.gz` — the 44,832-row snapshot
  corpus accumulated up to 2026-07-07, generated by the **pre-search-fix**
  champion (state evaluator returning 0.0 for most positions, frozen
  transposition-table rollouts; see `AUDIT_REPORT.md` §8.2). Retired per
  `docs/05-planning/CONTINUOUS_TRAINING_PLAN.md` P4: `heuristic_tune` fitting
  this corpus every night produced candidates *below* the champion (~45%).
  The live corpus at `data/champion_snapshots.csv` is regenerated from the
  fixed-search champion by the nightly data-refresh step
  (`training.data_refresh`) and is capped to the newest
  `scripts.champion_loop.SNAPSHOT_MAX_ROWS` rows. Do not train on this
  archive; it is kept only for before/after analysis.
